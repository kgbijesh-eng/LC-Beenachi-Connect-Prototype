## Mandatory prerequisites

- [ ] Cash is fully evidenced and reconciled.
- [ ] Every real bank account is separately evidenced and reconciled.
- [ ] Member receivables exactly equal the private obligation schedule.
- [ ] Every liability is independently evidenced.
- [ ] Every donor/external restriction and beneficiary obligation is independently evidenced.
- [ ] Every other opening account is independently evidenced.
- [ ] Historical economic meaning is documented.
- [ ] No residual difference remains.

Any unexplained residual difference blocks cutover. Opening equity must never be a balancing plug. Exactly one economically correct account may be selected only through a future DR-033 decision after all prerequisites and independent accounting review are complete.
