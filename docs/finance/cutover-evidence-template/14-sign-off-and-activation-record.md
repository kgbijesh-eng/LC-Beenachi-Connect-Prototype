## Separation-of-duties and professional-review controls

- [ ] Preparer is not the sole verifier or approver.
- [ ] Internal verification does not replace independent accounting review.
- [ ] Independent Chartered Accountant/accounting review is complete before activation.
- [ ] Creating this template did not perform or satisfy that review.
- [ ] No reviewer was appointed or inferred during template creation.
- [ ] Business and accounting reviewers jointly authorize only one specifically identified package.
- [ ] The technical operator executes only that specifically authorized package.
- [ ] A blank, unsigned, or partially approved package cannot activate.
