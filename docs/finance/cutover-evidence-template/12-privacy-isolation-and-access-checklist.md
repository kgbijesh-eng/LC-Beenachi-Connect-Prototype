Permanent provenance includes the non-sensitive manifest, versions, approvals/activation history, checksum, journal links, reversal/replacement links, and non-sensitive audit trail. Restricted supporting evidence is provisionally retained for eight completed Lions Years after cutover or longer when law, accounting advice, audit, dispute, investigation, restriction terms, unresolved beneficiary obligations, or legal hold requires it. Independent accounting validation of that period remains mandatory before activation.
