## 1. Decision dependencies

DR-026–DR-027 establish the prospective target but do not authorize package preparation until the remaining prerequisites are approved. DR-028–DR-040 must be decided before chart/source mapping approval. DR-041–DR-048 and DR-052 must all be decided before a dry run; the approved actor and review policies do not satisfy the still-Pending evidence, dry-run, and rollback decisions. DR-049–DR-051 constrain implementation and cannot be overridden by operational convenience.

## 2. Contradiction review

The approved qualifications and remaining recommendations do not contradict DR-001–DR-025. In particular, this register preserves Lions Year boundaries, 1 July cutover, separate real assets, fully allocated payments, immutable linked reversals, no synthetic statement balance, no default historical backfill, and DR-025 code normalization.

## 3. Approval recording rule

A future approval must record decision ID, selected option, rationale, owner, accounting reviewer where required, date, compatibility effect, historical-data effect, and document versions. The 18 approved rows above record the business-owner policy choices supplied on 19 July 2026; their required future accounting validations and package-specific approvals remain gates where stated. Every other row remains Pending. Partial verbal agreement or a policy approval does not activate an account, mapping, package, journal, API, or UI.

## Unsafe shortcuts rejected

The pending status cannot be bypassed by using frontend fund sections, receipt totals, current member ledgers, missing-as-zero assumptions, suspense balancing, generic bank accounts, mid-year dates, manual journals, or implementation defaults as business approval.
