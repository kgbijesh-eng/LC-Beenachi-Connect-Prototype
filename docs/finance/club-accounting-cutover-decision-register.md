## 1. Decision dependencies

DR-026–DR-027 establish the prospective target but do not authorize package preparation. DR-028 approves template semantics but exact source mappings and instances remain future gates. DR-033 remains Pending and blocks final opening-chart selection and package approval until reconciliation and independent accounting review identify exactly one economically correct equity account. DR-034 approves the reconciliation method, not actual schedules or totals. DR-046 approves mandatory outcomes, not an executable service or suite. DR-049–DR-051 constrain implementation and cannot be overridden by operational convenience.

## 2. Contradiction review

The approved qualifications and remaining DR-033 rules do not contradict DR-001–DR-025. In particular, this register preserves Lions Year boundaries, 1 July cutover, separate real assets, fully allocated payments, immutable linked reversals, no synthetic statement balance, no default historical backfill, DR-025 code normalization, and the prohibition on suspense, generic Other accounts, and direct manual posting.

## 3. Approval recording rule

A future approval must record decision ID, selected option, rationale, owner, accounting reviewer where required, date, compatibility effect, historical-data effect, and document versions. The 27 approved rows above record business-owner policy or internal design outcomes; their required future accounting validations, evidence, service designs, and package-specific approvals remain gates where stated. DR-033 remains Pending. Partial verbal agreement or a policy approval does not activate an account, mapping, package, journal, API, or UI. Internal system-design review is not Chartered Accountant certification, statutory advice, tax advice, or audit assurance.

## Unsafe shortcuts rejected

The pending status cannot be bypassed by using frontend fund sections, receipt totals, current member ledgers, missing-as-zero assumptions, suspense balancing, generic bank accounts, mid-year dates, manual journals, or implementation defaults as business approval.
