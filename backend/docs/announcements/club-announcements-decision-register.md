## Audience clarification

`club-members` means eligible active members of the owning club. `officers` means members satisfying a future authoritative club-scoped officer policy. An officer-audience record is not automatically a club-member record, and audience combination behavior is not approved.

## Content and time clarification

Plain Unicode text, title/body limits, UTC storage, and club-timezone display are approved with the qualifications recorded above. `Asia/Kolkata` may apply to LC Beenachi but is not a global SaaS default. Scheduled time is not publication, effective expiry is not scheduler evidence, and Urgent priority is not delivery. AN-003, AN-016, and AN-017 remain Pending and must not be inferred from these approvals.
